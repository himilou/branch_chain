Permits for Tree Work


Do I need a permit?

The answer to this depends on where you live.
Cities within King and Snohomish counties all have varying regulations regarding tree removal and trimming.

In general you need a permit if the following apply:

  • The tree has a trunk larger than 6" in diameter
  • The tree has branches larger than 4" in diameter
  • The tree is in a right-of-way or an easement
  • The tree is a protected species

Additional other factors may apply. For example Seattle has designated evironmentally critical areas due to slope and water runoff concerns.

Important:
Removing or pruning a tree in many cities around Puget Sound without a permit can lead to fines and \ or civil penalites.
It is important that you check with your local panning department prior to doing any work on your property, Even if its an emergency situation.
