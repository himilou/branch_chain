Winter Assessments

Winter Storms and High Winds

Get a winter assesment before winter arrives. Winter season in the Pacific Northwest brings high winds and often, heavy snow which can cause weak trees to fall or lose large branches.
Let us assess your property before a storm causes a fallen tree.

Storm Damage

If you just suffered a fallen tree from a storm call right away!

Fallen trees often end up resting on weak branches temporarily. Even a weak wind can cause them to fall further potentially causing even more damage to your property.

We have experience:

  • Securing trees to prevent further damage.
  • Working with rope and tackle to move tree sections to the ground safely.
  • Documenting damage for your insurance claim.
